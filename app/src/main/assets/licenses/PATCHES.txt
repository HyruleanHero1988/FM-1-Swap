FM-1 Swap patch banks

Banks 1–4
The FM-1's own presets, captured by KingParamount and shared as standard DX7
bank dumps. The SysEx capture is CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://github.com/KingParamount/fm1-factory-presets
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

KingParamount claims no rights in the voice parameters. Those come from
Yamaha ROM and VRC cartridges and from BlackWinny's Dexed_cart 1.0
collection, as selected and renamed by M-VAVE. The per-voice trace is in
that repository, docs/protocol-and-provenance.md.
