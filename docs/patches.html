FM-1 Swap

Patches you can send.

These are DX7 patches for an M-VAVE FM-1. SEND plays one. SEND BANK sends all 32, and you choose which bank on the FM-1 they replace. The phone app does the same thing from its PATCHES screen, over the USB cable. This page sends from a desktop browser that can see the FM-1.

Choose where the patch is going. Official V15 holds one patch until you press SAVE. FM-1+VA edits the preset you have selected, and you save it on the FM-1 to keep it. Official Open takes one patch onto its DX7 track.

Plug the FM-1 into this computer, then connect it.

FM-1 banks 1–4 are the instrument's own presets, captured by KingParamount and shared as DX7 banks. Aquanode's banks are CC0. Yamaha's cartridge banks are on fm1-editor.com. Not affiliated with M-VAVE.
